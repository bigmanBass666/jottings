## 提示词为动词

### 谓语

I went swimming in the local pool and started going to a gym. I then _____ (join) a group of runners. (2022-39) ✅
::: details answer
joined (该句无其他谓语, 所以空格填谓语)
:::

It ______ (mean) we have the chance to obtain information. ❌
::: details answer
mean, have, obtain 这三个都是动词, 那个是谓语呢? 我感觉是mean, 所以:  
mean ❌  
means ✅
需要注意主谓一致, 考点是三单
:::

It was a great honour to be invited backstage at the not-for-profit Panda Base, where ticket money helps pay for research, I ______ (allow) to get up close to these cute animals at the 600-acre centre. ✅
::: details answer
根据语意, 应该是被动: 被允许, so, be + done  
was allowed (god damn!)
:::

Since 2011, the country ______ (grow) more corn than rice. ✅
::: details answer
句子缺谓语, 看到了since, 感觉是现在完成时, 应该用have... 呃...忘了  

现在完成时: have/has done;  
过去完成时: had done;  
将来完成时: will have done;

1. 首先这句话是在现在说的, 所以一般现在时 -> has/have
2. grow-grew-**grown**
3. the country 三单 -> has

has grown ✅
:::

### 非谓语

She got off the bus, ______ (leave) her handbag on her seat. ✅
::: details answer
谓语是 got off?✅ 感觉不是被动, to do不太可能, 剩下doing  

leaving
:::

She got off the bus, but ______ (leave) her handbag on her seat. ✅
::: details answer
上一道题因为逗号不能连接两个句子, 所以逗号之后不是一个完整的句子, 自然也就没有谓语一说, 所以自然填的是非谓语.  
但这道题有了一个连词 but 连接, 所以逗号后面是一个完整的句子, leave作谓语

left
:::

For a long time I regretted not ______ (do) much sport. ✅
::: details answer
已有谓语 regretted , I 与 do 是主动关系, 显然不是to do

doing
:::

But Sarah, who has taken part in shows along with top models, wants ______ (prove) that she has brains as well as beauty. ✅
::: details answer
逗号不能连接两个句子, 所以逗号后面的句子是不完整的, 所以考非谓语  
Sarah 与 prove 是主动关系, 感觉进行时怪怪的, proving 太奇怪了, to do 通顺些

to prove ✅ (GAD DAMN!!!!💥💥💥💥)
:::

After ______ (spend) some time looking at all the defensive equipment at the wall, we decided it was time for some action. ✅
::: details answer ✨
谓语是 decided?, 主动, to do 不通顺 -> doing, 可是过去时态怎么办?

spending ✅ (holy sh*t!)

还有一个点: After是介词，介词后面接动名词ing，故填 spending。
:::

When he saw the mists rising from the river and the soft clouds ______ (surround) the mountain tops, he was reduced to tears. ✅
::: details answer
surrounding
:::

______ (strengthen) the connection with young people, the event included a number of public promotional activities on social media. ❌ <Badge type='danger' text='意思看不太懂'/>
::: details answer
Strengthening ❌  
To strengthen ✅
:::

The adobe dwellings ______ (build) by the Pueblo Indians of the American Southwest are admired by even the most modern of architects and engineers. ✅
::: details answer
dwellings ___ (build) by Pueblo are admired by ...  

predicate: admired; 被动 -> done -> build-built-built

built ✅ (太棒了哥)
:::

______ (Consider) the plan carefully, he rejected it. ❌
::: details answer
predicate: rejected  
主动, to do 太奇怪

Considering ❌  
Having considered ✅

由于要用完成时, 所以答案先是 have considered, 然后因为是主动, 所以having

非谓语的完成时​  
主动​  
having done-“已经”

被动​  
having been done-“已经被”
:::
