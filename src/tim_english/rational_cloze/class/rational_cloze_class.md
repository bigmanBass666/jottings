Read this story, ______ you will realize that not everything can be bought with money. ✅

and
:::

## 代词

**技巧**: 优先考虑it/its/one

Ecotourism has ______ (it) origin with the environmental movement of the 1970s. ✅  
::: details answer  
its
:::

## 副词

以下情况，用副词形式：

1. **修饰动词或动词短语**（如 cheer up）
2. **修饰形容词**
3. **修饰副词**
4. **修饰整个句子**

**技巧**：若发现空格需用副词，常将提示词加 **ly** 变副词。

Singles are flocking to the Internet ______ (main) because their busy lifestyles leave them little time. ✅  
::: details answer  
mainly
:::

## 名词

以下情况，用名词形式：

1. **在句中作主语**  
2. **在及物动词或介词（短语）后作宾语**  
3. **前面有冠词 a/an/the**  
4. **前面有形容词性物主代词**，如 **his, her, their, its** 等  
5. **前面有名词所有格修饰**，如 **my classmate’s, your girlfriend’s**

Their ______ (happy) is based on money. ✅  
::: details answer  
happiness
:::

## 介词

- **注意1**: 介词后接动名词ing
- **注意2**: 情态动词后接动词原形

| 技巧                     |                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 具体到某一天，用 on      | **on** Sunday                                                            |
| 具体到几点钟，用 at      | **at** 16:30                                                             |
| 与/和/对...伴随，用 with | be patient **with**<br>make friends **with**<br>get along well **with** 和睦相处 |
| 被动/方法/途径，用 by    | I was hurt **by** you.                                                   |
| 从...到...，用 from      | **from** **A** to B                                                          |
| ...的，用 of             | various types **of** trips                                               |
| 目的/原因，用 for        | do it **for** you                                                        |
| 作为/当作/像，用 as      | **as** a teacher                                                         |

You can keep healthy ______ consuming different categories of fresh foods.  
::: details answer  
by
:::

## 连词

**注意**：逗号不能连接两个句子，得用连词。

**技巧**：

- 连词题，优先考虑 **but, and, because, though/although, if, unless**
- **not...but** - “不是...而是...”
- 空格前的句子有 **not**，若考连词优先考虑 **but**；特别小心 **not** 被缩写了。

You can distinguish the twins very easily, ______ Tim is quiet while Jack is active. ✅  
::: details answer  
because
:::  

We were told that our rooms hadn't been reserved for that week, ______ for the week after.  
::: details answer  
but
:::

## 冠词

**技巧**: 空格后是名词或形容词+名词，优先考虑冠词a/an/the。

The truth is that this idea is ______ an unfortunate imaginary story. ❌  
::: details answer  
a ❌  
an ✅  只要无阻碍直接声带发声的都是元音
:::

## 名词复数

技巧-提示词若为名词，优先考虑变复数。

Several ______ (student) are playing basketball on the playground.  
::: details answer  
students
:::

## 比较等级

技巧1-看到than立刻考虑比较级。  
技巧2-看到比较级立刻考虑than。  
技巧3-看到最高级立刻考虑the。

In today’s class, he appeared to be ______ (active) than he used to be. ✅  
::: details answer  
more active
:::  

In today’s class, he appeared to be more active ______ he used to be.  
::: details answer  
than
:::

## 强调句

**技巧**：看到 **it is/was** 开头，立刻怀疑考强调句。

It was on his 22nd birthday ______ the young man received his first gift. ✅  
::: details answer  
that
:::

## 动词作谓语

技巧-提示词是动词且作谓语，优先考虑时态/被动/三单

Illegal hunting and trading of wild animals will ______ (punish) severely in China. ✅  
::: details answer ✨  
be punished (**情态动词后加动词原型**)
:::

## 动词作非谓语

**技巧**：提示词是动词且句子已有谓语，立刻考虑非谓语 **to do/ing/done**。

The children went home from the grammar school, their lessons ______ (finish) for the day. ❌  
::: details answer ✨  
were finished ❌  
finished ✅  
be done 是谓语, done才是非谓语
:::

## 过去时

**技巧**：看到 **yesterday, last, ago, then, just now, before** 等，优先考虑过去时。

The unmanned Chang’e 4 probe (探测器) the name was inspired by an ancient Chinese moon goddess ______ (touch) down last week in the South Pole-Aitken basin. ✅  
::: details answer  
touched
:::

### 注意 and

**技巧**：空格附近有 **and**，其前后的时态/单复数/非谓语等通常一致。

Confucius believed knives would remind people of killings and ______ (be) too violent for use at the table. ❌  
::: details answer ❗  
was ❌  
were ✅ (knives 是复数)
:::

## 现在完成时

**技巧 1**：by + 现在的时间/动作 = 用现在完成时。

**技巧 2**：since 时间，for + 时间段。

**技巧 3**：over the past/last + 时间段  
             in the past/last + 时间段。

**注意**：认识单词 decade “十年”。

**技巧 4**：在句型 It is the first/second time that...，从句用现在完成时。

**技巧 5**：看到 already, yet, ever, just, so far, recently, up to now 可优先考虑现在完成时。

In the last few years, China ______ (make) great achievements in environment protection. ❌
::: details answer
have made ❌  
has made ✅  
注意三单
:::

He told me that he ______ (be) here for ten minutes. ❌
::: details answer
was been ❌  
had been ✅  
结构记错了哥
:::
