# 主谓一致

p81

## 人称与数的一致原则  

1. **第三人称单数主语 + 单数谓语**  
   - He loves reading.  
   - She writes novels.  

2. **复数主语 + 复数谓语**  
   - They love classical music.  
   - We study together.  

## 特殊计量单位  

表示时间、金钱、距离、重量的复数名词，**整体视为单数**：  

1. **金钱类**  
   - Two million dollars is big money.  
   - Fifty yuan is enough for lunch.  

2. **时间类**  
   - Two days is not a long time to wait.  
   - Ten years has passed quickly.  

3. **距离与重量类**  
   - Fifty kilometers is his daily cycling distance.  
   - Three kilograms is the weight limit.  

## 易错辨析  

当具体指代多个独立单位时，**谓语用复数**：  

- These two days have been exhausting.  
- The dollars were scattered on the table.  
- The kilometers they ran were recorded separately.  

## 谓语用单数

- **复合不定代词作主语**
  - Nothing is impossible.

- **不定式、动名词作主语**
  - To read is to learn.
  - Reading is fun.

- **many a/the whole/each/every/either/neither+名词**
  - Many a student has read the book.
  - The whole story is interesting.
  - Each book is useful.
  - Every student is diligent.
  - Either answer is correct.
  - Neither suggestion is perfect.

## The + 形容词/分词

- **表示一类人或一类物**
  - 谓语动词常用复数。
  - 例如：The wounded are my friends.

- **表示个人或某类事物**
  - 谓语用单数。
  - 例如：The wounded is my friend.

## the/a number of 对比  

- **the number of + 复数名词**（表示数量）→ **单数谓语**  
  - The number of students is 50.  

- **a number of + 复数名词**（表示许多）→ **复数谓语**  
- A number of books are missing from the shelf.  

## 就远原则  

- **谓语与主要主语保持一致**  
  - Yanzu, together with Jacky, goes to school.  
  - The manager, along with his team, is attending the meeting.  

- **连接词列表**  
  with, together with, along with, as well as  

## 就近原则  

谓语与最靠近它的主语保持主谓一致  

- **Either...or...**（两者中的任何一个）  
  - Either Tom or his brothers **are** responsible.  
  - Either the cake or the cookies **need** refrigeration.  

- **Neither...nor...**（两者都不）  
  - Neither John nor his friends **know** the answer.  
  - Neither the teacher nor the student **has** arrived.  

- **Not only...but also...**（不仅...而且...）  
  - Not only the students but also the teacher **enjoys** the film.  
  - Not only the book but also the pens **are** on the desk.  

- **There be 结构**  
  - There **is** a book and two pens on the table.  
  - There **are** two pens and a book on the table.  

## 注意事项解析  

- **结构对比**  
  - **The poet and the writer**（重复冠词）→ 指代两个不同个体  
    - 中文意义：诗人和作家（2人）  
    - 例句：The poet and the writer **support** the proposal.  

  - **The poet and writer**（单冠词）→ 指代同一人兼具双重身份  
    - 中文意义：诗人兼作家（1人）  
    - 例句：The poet and writer **is** attending the conference.  

**解题思路**  

1. 观察冠词重复性 → 判断指代人数  
2. 根据人数 → 确定谓语单复数  

## 集体名词用法  

- **核心规则**  
  - **表整体（单数谓语）**  
    - My family **is** very supportive. （家庭作为整体）  
  - **表个体成员（复数谓语）**  
    - The police **are** searching for the suspect. （警察作为个体）  

- **常见集体名词列表**  
  - family, class, team, audience, government  

**解题思路**  

1. 判断句子强调重点 → 整体单位 / 个体成员  
2. 选择对应谓语形式
