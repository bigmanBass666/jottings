::: details answer
are ❌ (Because I think "more privacy" is means multiple privacy. So I use plural);  
are ❌ (same reason)

Mr. Smith has bought a little house in the country, around which ______ (be) some green trees. ❌
::: details answer
was ❌  
are ✅

around which are some green trees是一个由“介词+which”引出的定语从句，在该从句中，主语是some green trees，而around which是表语，所以句子谓语应用are而不是is。

史密斯先生在乡下买了一座小房子，房子周围都是绿树。
:::
