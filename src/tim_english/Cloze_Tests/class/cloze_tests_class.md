# 完形填空

《复习全书·新题型》P241  

## 变革年 2015 年  

前-20 空-0.5 分共 10 分  
后到 2021-15 空-1 分共 15 分  
现-2022-15 空-2 分-30 分  

## 考点变化  

前-重词汇搭配语法轻逻辑  
后-重逻辑轻词汇搭配语法  

## 做题技巧  

- 无需通读全文，直接按顺序往下做题  
- 不要关注不懂的表达，要关注懂的表达

## 2021年真题

The idea of becoming a writer occurred to Nicole when she was meeting with some friends in a bar. "If you could do anything, \_\_1\_\_ cost, location, and education being factors, what would you do?" One of them asked \_\_2\_\_. The table went \_\_3\_\_ as everyone was set to thinking.

Like all of her friends in the \_\_4\_\_, Nicole was a mom with two little kids. She loved being a mom, \_\_5\_\_ the busy life of taking care of two children made her \_\_6\_\_ that being a full-time mom was the \_\_7\_\_ for her now. Eventually, her children would be \_\_8\_\_ to college and then her purpose would be fulfilled, she thought.

1. A. without  B. by  C. as  D. of
::: details 1 ✅
A
:::

2. A. happily  B. clearly  C. suddenly  D. slowly
::: details 2 ✅
C
:::

3. A. crazy  B. silent  C. suddenly  D. wild
::: details 3 ✅
B
:::

4. A. house  B. restaurant  C. bar  D. room  
::: details 4 ✅  
C
:::

5. A. because  B. when  C. or  D. but  
::: details 5 ✅  
D
:::

6. A. like  B. choose  C. realize  D. wonder  
::: details 6 ✅  
C
:::

7. A. idea  B. chapter  C. stereotype  D. choice  
::: details 7 ❌  
C ❌  
D ✅
:::

8. A. forward  B. off  C. along  D. down  
::: details 8 ❌  
A ❌  
B. off ✅ (有"离开", "断开"的意思在里头)
:::
