Dear Mr.smith,

__Hello! I am Li Hua, one of your students. I am very delighted to tell you that we are going to hold a theme party for the Dragon Boat Festival. I am writing to invite you to join us.  

__Here are the details. In the first place, the party will begin at June 3 8pm. What's more, the place of party is at 306 class of teaching department. Last but not least, there are some exciting activities by then. For instance, making ZongZi, telling story and reading poem, and so forth.  

__I suggest that you should prepare a show. Would you please let me know whether you would be willing to join us? I would be grateful if you could accept my invitation. With my best wishes to you.  
  
                                                                        Yours,  
                                                                        Li Hua

## reflection

- 班长: monitor
- 班会: class meeting
- 临近sth: sth is coming
- 列举活动那里要用复数: telling stories, reciting poems
- 活动将在某个时间被举行: The class meeting will be held `on June 3 at 8 p.m.`
- 在某间教室, 教学楼或某某楼的表达: in `classroom 306` of the teaching `building`
  > 注意`classroom`和`building`

## model essay

Dear Mr. Smith,

I'm Li Hua, the monitor. The Dragon Boat Festival is coming. We are going to hold a class meeting. All the teachers and students will attend the meeting. We would like to invite you to our class meeting. I promise you'll be very interested in it.

There will be a great number of different activities in the meeting including making zongzi, telling stories and reciting poems and so on. The class meeting will be held on June 3 at 8 p.m. in classroom 306 of the teaching building.

Finally, I would be extremely delighted if you accept my invitation and I'm sure you'll enjoy the class meeting very much.

Yours,  
Li Hua
