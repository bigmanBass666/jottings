Dear Dr. Cooper,

__I am Li Hua, the president of the `student union`(Student Union✅). `The Chinese New Year is coming`(❌可改进), I am very delighted to tell you that our school `are`(is✅) going to hold a Chinese New Year Culture Fair. We would like to invite you to our Fair.

__There will be a great deal of different activities in the fair including write couplets, paper cutting and make dumplings and so forth. The fair will be held on December 29 at 3 p.m. in school auditorium.

__Would you please let me know whether you would be willing to join us? I `will`(would✅) be grateful if you could accept my invitation. With my best wishes to you.

                                                                        Yours,  
                                                                        Li Hua

## reflection

- 学生会首字母要大写: Student Union
- "The Chinese New Year is coming" can be improved to "As The Chinese New Year approaches"

## model essay

Dear Dr. Cooper,​​

I am Li Hua, president of the Student Union. As the ​Chinese New Year approaches, I'm delighted to **announce** that our school will hold ​a "Chinese New Year Culture Fair" at 3 pm on December 29 in the school auditorium. `We cordially invite you to join us!`

The fair will feature ​traditional activities including:

- ​Writing Spring Festival couplets​
- ​Creating paper cuttings​
- ​Making dumplings​

​Would you like to demonstrate​ a cultural tradition from your country? (✨ 替换生硬的建议句式)  
​Please confirm your participation by December 25​ so we can prepare accordingly. (⏰ 明确回复期限)

Looking forward to celebrating with you!

​Yours sincerely,​​  
​Li Hua
