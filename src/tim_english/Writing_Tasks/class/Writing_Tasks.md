# 写作

p249

## 第一段

**背景**

- 高兴/遗憾
  - I am very delighted/sad to tell you that...
  - I am very delighted/sad to know that...

**目的**

- I am writing to...
  - apologize to you for
  - say thanks to you for
  - say congratulations to you for
  - complain to you for
  - invite you to
  - apply for
  - offer my opinions on

**回信的开头**

- I'm writing in response to your last letter about...
- 这是给你关于......的回信。

## 第二段

**总起句**

- Here are the reasons/details/suggestions.

**分两/三点**

- 第一 In the first place,
- 第二 What's more,（好） what's worse,（坏）
- 第三 Last but not least,
- 例如 for instance,
- 等等 and so forth.

## 第三段

**建议**

- I suggest that you should+动词原形
- I suggest that you should take some effective measures to deal with the problem.

## 客套话

- With my best wishes to you.
- Would you please let me know whether you would be willing to...
- 烦请告知您是否愿意......
- Please inform me if you need any other information.
- 若需要任何其他信息请让我知悉。
- I am looking forward to your early reply.
- 期待您的早日回复。（注意上下文）

## 客套话-如果您能接受我的邀请，我将非常开心，祝您幸福

- I would be grateful if you could accept my invitation.
- With my best wishes to you.
