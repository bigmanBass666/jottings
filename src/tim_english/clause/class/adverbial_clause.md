# 状语从句

学习目标-掌握对应的连词意思及写法即可。  
《复习全书·新题型》- 连词+状语从句章节

## 常考连词技巧  

### 基础连词

And · but · if · unless “除非”  

### 让步关系

though · although  

### 表原因

because · for · since  

## 随堂练习

______ you behave yourself, you shall not go to the party this afternoon. ✅
::: details answer
Unless (做过, 所以才看出来的)
:::

The son promised that he would **return** in five years, ______ he didn't. ✅
::: details answer
but
:::
