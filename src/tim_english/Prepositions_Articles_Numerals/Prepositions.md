# 介词-复习全书 p57

## 一

He flew 4,700 kilometers ____ Xi'an to Kashgar. ✅
::: details answer
from ✅
:::

____ receiving the book, she went over the most interesting chapters first. ❌
::: details answer 🤖
In ❌

答案: On

on除了表示“在..上”，还可表示“就在...之后，一.就”; 此外空格后的动词用了动名词，也可提醒此处很可能用介词。

翻译: 一收到书她就首先看了那些最有趣的章节。

## 六

Jane received an offer from a big company, and Joan was a little bit jealous ____ her. 🟨
::: details answer
of 🟨
:::

Although he had failed in driving test many times, he insisted ____ taking the test again. ❌ <Badge type="danger" text="搭配" />

to ❌

:::
