## 回信

### 首段

**I am exceedingly delighted when receiving your email**, asking me about the [school gym]. I'm `more than happy` to introduce it to you. -- [(via)](../Writing_Tasks/book/my_answer/2021.md#model-essay)

## 临近sth/sth即将到来

- as sth approaches (As The Chinese New Year approaches)
- sth is coming

## 邀请信

As [节日名] approaches, I'm delighted to announce that [活动名称] will be held at [时间] in [地点]. **On behalf of** [班级/组织], I cordially invite you to join us!  

### 邀请你加入

We cordially invite you to join us!  
我们诚挚地邀请您加入我们！

### 您的参与将为活动增色

Your participation would greatly enrich our `cultural` experience.  

## 包含这些活动

The `fair` will feature `​traditional` activities including: ...  
The `event` will feature `a variety of traditional` activities, such as: ...

## 首先 其次 等等 例如

- 第一 In the first place, first and foremost
- 第二 What's more,（好） what's worse,（坏）
- 第三 Last but not least,
- 例如 for instance,
- 等等 and so forth.
- All in all: 总的来说
