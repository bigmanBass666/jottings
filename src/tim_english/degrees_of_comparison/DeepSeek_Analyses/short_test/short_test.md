## **一、规则变化填空**

1. **bright** → ______ → ______  
2. **safe** → ______ → ______  
3. **heavy** → ______ → ______  
4. **thin** → ______ → ______  
5. **important** → ______ → ______  

## **二、不规则变化选择**

1. Which is correct?  
   A. bad → badder → baddest  
   B. bad → worse → worst  
   C. bad → more bad → most bad  

2. "She feels ______ today." (ill)  
   A. iller  
   B. more ill  
   C. worse  

3. "He has ______ books than me." (many)  
   A. much  
   B. more  
   C. manyer  

4. "This is the ______ cake I’ve ever eaten!" (good)  
   A. goodest  
   B. best  
   C. better  

## **三、语法改错**

1. She is the most happiest person in the room.  
2. This box is much heavy than that one.  
3. I have less friends than you.  

## **四、综合应用题**

翻译以下句子（注意比较级和最高级）：  

1. 黄河是中国第二长的河流。  
2. 这本书比那本书有趣得多。  
3. 他是我们班最聪明的学生之一。
