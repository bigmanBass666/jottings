# Now more and more people are busy ____ (learn) about the Internet

## 🌟 彻底解析：为什么填 **learning**？

### 🔍 **核心语法规则**

1. **固定搭配**：**be busy doing sth**（忙于做某事）  
   - 此处必须用 **动名词（-ing形式）** 作宾语  
   - 例：  
     > She is busy **working** on the project.  
     > They were busy **preparing** for the exam.  

2. **错误答案分析**  
   - 若填 **to learn**（不定式）❌：  
     > *be busy to do* 是错误搭配，不符合英语表达习惯  

---

### ⚡ **高频考点总结**

#### 📚 类似用法的形容词/动词短语  

| 表达          | 正确结构                 | 例句                          |  
|--------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|  
| be busy      | be busy **doing**        | He's busy **cooking** dinner. |  
| be worth     | be worth **doing**       | The movie is worth **watching**. |  
| have fun     | have fun **doing**       | We had fun **playing** games. |  

---

### 📝 **强化训练**

1. **She spent the whole day ______ (clean) the house.**  
::: details answer  
cleaning  
（花整天打扫房子 | 固定搭配：spend time **doing**）  
:::  

2. **The book is worth ______ (read) twice.**  
::: details answer  
reading  
（值得读两遍 | be worth + 动名词）  
:::  

3. **They had difficulty ______ (find) the address.**  
::: details answer  
finding  
（难以找到地址 | have difficulty **doing**）  
:::
