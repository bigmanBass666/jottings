# 倒装

p185

Never before ______ she seen anybody who can play tennis as well as Robert. ❌  
::: details answer  
does ❌  
has ✅  
never否定词位于句首，句子要使用倒装语序，根据句意判断可知是从过去一直到现在的情况，用现在完成时。
:::

Not until he retired from teaching three years ago ______ he consider having a holiday abroad. ✅  
::: details answer  
did  (three years ago 可看出是过去时)
:::

This is not my story, nor ______ it the whole story. ❌  
::: details answer 🤖  
does ❌  
is ✅  
[前句 ​"This is not..." 使用 ​be动词 (is) → 后句需用同类动词！](https://yuanbao.tencent.com/bot/app/share/chat/IFanQKmARgTJ)
:::

Seldom ______ I make any mistakes during my past few years of working. ❌  
::: details answer 🤖  
have ❌  
did ✅  
past few years提醒需用过去时，  
[​during my past few years of working → ​明确过去时间段​（已结束）→ ​需用一般过去时！](https://yuanbao.tencent.com/bot/app/share/chat/xxkWZ1YCejam)
:::

Little ______ he know that this region was so rich in natural resources. ❌  
::: details answer  
dose ❌ (我看到know, 所以我感觉是现在时)  
did ✅  
其实倒装结构本来就是要加 **动词原形** , 所以还要根据从句的时态来确定主句的时态
:::

No sooner ______ his luggage been loaded than the bus started off. ✅  
::: details answer  
had
:::

Seldom ______ we been treated in such a rude way. ❌
::: details answer 🤖
had ❌  
have ✅  
[​Seldom/Rarely/Never 等词在句首时，必须用 ​现在完成时（have）​ 或 ​一般过去时（did）​，具体取决于时间范围](https://yuanbao.tencent.com/bot/app/share/chat/cwfc94mmbHUj)
:::

She never laughed, nor ______ she ever lose her temper. ✅
::: details answer
我看到laughed是过去时, 所以我填:  
did ✅ (6)
:::

We mention the importance of giving up smoking once and again, but hardly ______ they realized it. ❌
::: details answer 🤖
realized, 所以过去时:  
did ❌  
have ✅  
[​主句的现在时（mention）暗示动作持续至今，因此从句需用现在完成时（have）表达结果的一致性。](https://yuanbao.tencent.com/bot/app/share/chat/1kB3BzUeuYKg)
:::

has ✅ 💥💥💥
:::
