# 倒装

p185

## 示例

**原句**：She hardly has time to listen to music.  
**倒装**：Hardly does she have time to listen to music.  

**原句**：He seldom takes a shower in winter.  
**倒装**：Seldom does he take a shower in winter.  

## 倒装技巧

**把助动词放到主语前**  

助动词包括：  

- be 动词  
- 情态动词  
- do 类  
- have 类  

## 助动词的来源

**原句若有，则用同类；若无，则用 do 类。**

| 原句                     | 倒装                     |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| You are a boy.           | Are you a boy?           |
| You have a friend.       | Do you have a boyfriend? |
| You have had a friend.   | Have you had a friend?   |

## 否定型倒装

- **否定词在句首引起倒装**  
  否定词 = hardly, seldom, rarely, never, no, not, nor, little, few, neither, none...

## only 型倒装

- **only 位于句首引起倒装**  
  **Only when I sleep will I make a dream.**

## So do I 型

- **So do I.** 我也...  
- **So I do.** 我确实...  

**注意**：do 表示助动词，I 表示主语。  

**示例**：  

- She likes Yanzu. So do I.  
  她喜欢彦祖，我也一样。  
- She likes Yanzu. So she does.  
  她喜欢彦祖，她确实喜欢。

## 随堂练习

Little ______ he know that this region was so rich in natural resources. ❌  
::: details answer  
does ❌  
did ✅  
思路是正确的, 但是还需注意到整个句子的时态, 该题的"was"表明该句是过去时
:::

Only under special circumstances ______ freshman permitted to take make-up tests. ✅ <Badge type='tip' text='人类智慧闪耀时'/>  
::: details answer  
因为我看到 permitted, 所以我吸取教训, 填:  
did ❌

但我又想了想, 这是个被动语态, 所以是be done结构, 所以原句是有助动词be的, 而且从take可以推断出freshman是复数, 所以是are, 空格应该填:  
are ✅
:::

California relies heavily on income from fruit crops, so ______ Florida. ✅  
::: details answer  
do ❌  
does ✅ (因为Florida是三单, relies是现在时)
:::
