# In terms of artistic value, it is hard to say that one country's art form is greater than ____ of another. ❌

::: details answer
the other ❌  
that ✅  
艺术价值方面，很难说一个国家的艺术形式比另一个国家的更伟大。
:::

## 关键语法点：**替代词 "that" 的用法**  

**正确答案**：**that**  
**错误选项**：the other  

---

### **深度解析：**

#### 1. **替代词 "that" 的功能**  

- **替代不可数名词或单数可数名词**：当比较对象是 **同一类事物的不同个体** 时，用 **that** 替代前文提到的名词以避免重复。  
  - 例句：  
    - The **climate** of Japan is milder than **that** of Canada.  
      （日本的**气候**比加拿大的**气候**温和 → **that** 替代 **climate**）  

#### 2. **为什么不能用 "the other"？**  

- **语义不符**：  
  - **the other** 表示 **两者中的另一个**，但句子中比较的是 **多个国家中的任意一个**（未限定总数），且需替代的是 **art form**（艺术形式），而非国家本身。  
  - ❌ 错误逻辑：  
    **...greater than the other of another** → 暗示“另一个国家的另一个艺术形式”，逻辑混乱。  

---

### **对比练习：**

| 错误句 | 正确句 | 关键差异 |
|--------|--------|----------|
| The **population** of China is larger than **the other** of India. | The **population** of China is larger than **that** of India. | "that" 替代不可数名词 "population" |
| His **idea** is more creative than **the other** of his colleague. | His **idea** is more creative than **that** of his colleague. | "that" 替代单数名词 "idea" |

---

### **语法规则总结**

1. **替代不可数名词或单数名词** → **that**  
   - 例句：  
     The **history** of Rome is longer than **that** of Paris.  
2. **替代复数名词** → **those**  
   - 例句：  
     The **streets** here are cleaner than **those** in the city center.  

---

### **自测练习**

1. The **culture** of this tribe is more unique than ____ of modern societies.  
::: details 答案  
**答案：that**（替代不可数名词 "culture"）  
:::  

2. The **buildings** in ancient towns are often older than ____ in new cities.  
::: details 答案  
**答案：those**（替代复数名词 "buildings"）  
:::  

3. Her **voice** is sweeter than ____ of any other singer.  
::: details 答案  
**答案：that**（替代单数名词 "voice"）  
:::
